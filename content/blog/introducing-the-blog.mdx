This is the new home for news and longer-form writing about Stellarium: release notes, design discussions, and walkthroughs of what people are building on the network.

## Writing a post

Posts are MDX files in `content/blog/`. Each one exports its title, description, and publish date, then continues as ordinary markdown:

```mdx
export const metadata = {
  title: "My post",
  description: "One sentence for the index and search results.",
  date: "2026-10-26",
};

Regular **markdown** goes here.
```

The blog index lists posts newest first by `date`.
